# Research notes

Checked 2026-11-19.

- Lendscape sells a receivables finance platform to banks and lenders. Earlypay, an Australian listed invoice financier, selected Lendscape in 2025 "for its ability to consolidate three legacy systems onto one platform" and has completed the move, building its own client portal on top (ABF Journal, 28 August 2026: https://www.abfjournal.com/earlypay-moves-invoice-finance-business-onto-lendscape-platform/).
- Lendscape publishes no price list.
- Other platforms for invoice financiers quote privately too: FactorAvenue ("customized pricing based on your factoring operations", https://factoravenue.com/) and FactorView ("Pricing is built around your invoice volume and team size", https://factorview.com/pricing).
- The law cited in docs/compliance.md was read from the Personal Property Securities Act 2009 (Cth), Compilation No. 22: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00130/latest/text
