---
description: Our ledger against the client's own aged receivables: invoices the debtor may have paid to the client, amounts that differ, invoices never assigned.
---

Ask for the client's aged receivables export as CSV (Xero, MYOB or similar). Run `npm run finance -- reconcile <client> <file> --json`. Lead with invoices open with us but paid or missing in theirs: that is where misdirected payments and credit notes hide.
